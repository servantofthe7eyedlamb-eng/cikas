Craftsmanship

Made with Care

Every Cikas bottle is 3D-printed, layer by layer, into a detailed, sculpted design. Bold “Cikas” lettering sits on the front, a simple cross is cut clean through the bottle, and the back is covered in a raised scroll pattern.

  • 3D-printed, detailed bottle
  • Bold “Cikas” lettering
  • Simple cutout cross
  • Raised scroll pattern
  • Available in white, tan and green
